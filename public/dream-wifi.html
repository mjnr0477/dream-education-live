Next steps

Connect a real existing hotspot

IronWiFi manages authentication; it does not supply the internet connection or make unrelated public hotspots available automatically. The network owner must approve Dream's use and configure their existing access point or controller.

  1. Open the provider console and confirm whether the account can create a network and captive portal.
  2. Ask a participating venue or hotspot operator to authorize a test on its existing equipment.
  3. Configure the captive portal for that exact access point/controller and test a real phone from join through internet access and session expiry.
Open IronWiFi Console ↗ Check compatible devices ↗ Captive portal guide ↗

Important: a successful API check does not itself provide internet. To finish the real connection, Dream still needs a participating network owner/provider, a configured captive portal, and an authorized access point or controller for end-to-end device testing. Packages and payments stay disabled until those steps are verified.
